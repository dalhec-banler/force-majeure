# ADR-0024: The arsenal arrives on the calendar, stands up on the chest, and can be lost

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-08-26 (author: "you shouldn't have enough
funds at the beginning to use every weapon … as you build a treasure
chest, that is what unlocks weapons, and poor performance means your
funds go away and so do the weapons")

- A wing is **ordered** (click it in the tray) once its year has come and
  the chest holds its threshold; it stands up at the next review and
  charges upkeep for as long as it is kept. The lab wings (upkeep ≤ $1M)
  stand themselves up.
- **Mothballed by attrition** after four seasons under a quarter of its
  chest; **by order** with ⏏ on the tray. It reopens at three-quarters of
  the chest. Always a way back.
- **Earmarks stand wings up**: the committee's demonstration money
  (aerosol 1984 $40M; flagship 1995, 2015, 2035) stands the wing up
  whether or not the chest could; if the demonstration never flies the
  wing goes back to the desert with the money.
- Locked tools stay on the tray, dimmed, with the year or the chest
  they need — the passive lesson. The briefing card and a Budget-office
  memo say what the wing is waiting for.
- The rival flies the century's technology: no capability before its year.

## Consequences
The opening is a lab with seeding planes and a budget line; by ENMOD a
programme has two or three wings; the full arsenal's rent ($43M) needs a
frightened committee. Nothing in the tutorial decade can move a market
yet — the "Move a market" directive waits for 1966 and asks for the wing.
