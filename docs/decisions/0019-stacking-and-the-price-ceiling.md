# ADR-0019: A stack is a pattern; the sky cannot be veiled six times; grain has a ceiling

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-08-26 (audit night; from the strategist's 60-campaign sweep)

## Context
The dominant strategy found overnight: several Stratospheric Aerosol
Injections in one season, repeated every ~10 seasons. Same-season landings
escaped the forensics repeat multiplier (each paid base signature), the
grain price had no ceiling (reached 534 = 5× baseline), and the resulting
severity drove the mandate to its cap so each wave paid for the next.
Best valid score 493; a nine-stack reached 573 and exposed itself on the
final row.

## Decision (all gated; the sheet baseline is unchanged)
1. **Same-season landings count as pattern evidence** (forensics): a
   landing's repeat count includes the player's signature landings on the
   same target earlier in the same season; the multiplier cap rises 4 → 6.
2. **Stacked driver injections saturate** (forensics): the k-th identical
   player driver injection landing in one season adds 0.65^(k−1) of its
   magnitude.
3. **Grain price ceiling** `priceCap` (console: 300 = 3× baseline). Real
   crisis spikes (1973, 2008) ran 2–3×.
4. Directives: "Show the flag" pays $30M (it asks for a $26M op); "Stay
   invisible" asks for dossier under 40.

## Consequences
- Post-fix rerun of the strategist's campaigns: monsoonF 396 (valid,
  dossier 194), researchstrike 283, ensoflag 210, fortress 197; the
  fourteen-adaptation and tempest/veilshield variants now expose
  themselves. The wave is still the best play; it is no longer free.
- Still open (author): hubs count in the grain supply index (semiconductor
  output moves the wheat price); single-region drying moves price ~2% per
  op, so the "wheat exporter" archetype is weak at 36 regions; cheap play
  is unviable (idle income ≈ overhead + $4M); the rival never mattered to a
  strong player; Climate Research has no score effect (its payoff is
  legibility, by design — but nothing rewards knowing the wiring).
