# ADR-0022: The Eastern Program grows up with the century

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-08-26 (author: "Rival does need teeth, this
should increase as you move into contemporary times tho, and be almost
irrelevant in the beginning of game play")

## Decision (engine opt `rivalEras`, default off — the season-count schedule stays for the harness baseline)
The rival's schedule is keyed to the **calendar year**, never the season
count:

| era | years | the Eastern Program |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1946–1961 | does not exist. Nothing. |
| 1 | 1962–1975 | a lab with seeding planes: seeds its own steppe every 10 seasons. That is all. |
| 2 | 1976–1999 | ENMOD-era operator: homeland watershed every 7, favourite-harvest hits, an Atlantic forcing a decade, off-cycle hits on a passive programme; **retaliation at rung 5** every 4 |
| 3 | 2000–2029 | contemporary: homeland every 5, adds a stratospheric veil a decade; retaliation every 3 |
| 4 | 2030– | the future: homeland every 4, adds an ENSO forcing a decade; retaliation every 2 |

## Consequences
- In the current 10-year prototype slice (1946–55) the rival is absent by
  design; it arrives with the long campaign (ADR-0023 pending).
- The rival's ops are the cover the world gives you; a quiet early world
  means fewer strike windows and a slower first decade — intended: the
  opening is a lab with a budget line.
