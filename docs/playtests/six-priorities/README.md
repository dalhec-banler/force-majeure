# Six-priority development pass

Completed on the `development/visible-consequences` branch, September 4–5, 2026. Entry point: `prototype/web/console.html`. Choose **PLAY THE SIXTEEN-REVIEW CRISIS** in the operating brief, or keep the long record. This is a playable development build; no public deployment was made.

## Design intent

Preserve the existing satellite globe, phosphor terminal, IBM Plex typography, flat ruled panels, restrained green/amber/red instrumentation and dry bureaucratic voice. The world stays the dominant surface. Hierarchy: target and present condition, known next-season forcing, operation commitment, eventual outcome. Regional details live in the existing scrollable intelligence rail. No new decorative card system. Use existing spacing, thin borders and typography. Weather and aftermath belong on the Earth texture; labels belong in the instrument layer.

## Delivered

1. **Reliable rules and replay.** Targets validate before charging. Adaptation stops at 90%, with the final six points costing $9M; excess orders are refused. Completed regional research is refused. Undrawn earmarks cannot fund containment or open every listed wing. Driver stacking discounts persist through burn-down. Positive hurricane forcing is not classified as benign rain. Save reconstruction consumes pending wing orders identically to live play. Homeland selection no longer mutates a shared model. Known forecasts apply natural decay and known jet consequences. History now follows DJF/MAM/JJA/SON, including exact leap-year fractions. One December 1945 storm now correctly belongs to Winter 1946; no old storm IDs were removed.
2. **Strategic incentives.** Repeated seeding counts as useful committee work only with an observed or known forecast drought. Protection and research also count. Export participation and production weight affect national revenue. The programme's windfall share is 0.65, down from 2. The rival has a capped $90M budget, $5M seasonal replenishment after activation, a three-season intervention interval, and conditional home relief or watershed pressure. Queued wing upkeep is reserved in spendable funds. This is a deterministic conditional opponent, not a search-based AI.
3. **Readable consequences.** Selected-region wires are the default; selected/all/off remain available. Regions can be selected from the globe or a native keyboard-accessible list. Intelligence pairs known forcing without orders with forcing including orders, states uncertainty, and lists connection strengths and delays. Explicit directive targets have locate buttons. Field reports show commitment cost, landing date, realized output and own forcing, or protection/research results. Driver reports name known downstream destinations and delays. Pending and active effects retain their transit board.
4. **Visual aftermath.** The previous rejected rain blobs remain replaced by satellite-cloud texture formation in the Earth shader. Added textured burn/smoke, crop damage, inundation stress and persistent protection overlays read resolved state; existing drought, driver, polar and orbital effects remain. No new screen-space falling rain. These are stylized regional satellite cues, not terrain-resolved fire or hydrology models. Biology now damages output independently of rain. The AMOC operation continues for the rest of the campaign. Reduced motion fixes weather phase rather than moving it; WebGL-unavailable devices retain instrument reports.
5. **Useful archive.** Free-text predictions are safely preserved beside review-close outcomes. The UI no longer promises automatic scoring of prose. Three largest revenue departures, incremental revenue, operation/containment spend and the shadow-world assumptions are explicit. Up to twenty local tenure summaries support comparisons for the same nation, format and rules. The shadow's rival may respond differently because player orders are absent. No metagame stat bonuses.
6. **A complete short campaign.** Sixteen seasonal reviews, 2030–33, eight available wings, $120M emergency start for each nation, drought → export squeeze → wet phase → recovery. Finish all sixteen without exposure, insolvency or dissolution, average at least 85% homeland output over the last four seasons, and retain $100M. It has its own save slot, briefing, stage guidance and ending. The long record remains available. Rules revision 3 warns before reconstructing old saves.

Additional playtest fixes: drought stress no longer calls a 90% harvest a collapse; the 90-second timer formats as 1:30; intro fade stops intercepting clicks; operation controls disable during resolution; old “run anything” / “a seed a year” advice was removed.

- `python3 prototype/web/build.py`: self-contained console about **3.99 MB**.
- `git diff --check`: clean.
- `node prototype/web/tests/simulate-campaigns.js`: **35 deterministic policy campaigns**, recorded in [campaigns.json](campaigns.json). Twenty-five crisis cases across five nations and five policies; ten long cases. These selected policies are not a statistical estimate of player win rates or proof of optimal balance. All five national starts have a tested recovery strategy using measured protection spending and mothballing unused wings. All five homeland-only seeding policies eventually dissolve in the long record; regional relief policies complete it.

## Hands-on browser playtests

Three full runs through ordinary visible controls in connected Chrome at `http://127.0.0.1:8473/console.html`, with 1440×900 visual inspection and an additional 1200×800 archive check. Builds were refreshed during iteration; screenshots document the stage at which they were taken.

1. **Recovery run, 16 reviews.** Protection in reviews 1, 7 and 13; seeding in 2 and 3; five unused wings mothballed from review 3. Finished with $244.5M, last-four homeland output 95.0%, incremental homeland revenue +$10.3M. Checked cloud formation, locate control, known dry/wet projections, reserve spending, predictions and the successful ending. [Clouds](clouds.png), [archive](archive.png).
2. **Destructive diagnostic run, 16 reviews.** Deliberately burned the homeland during drought three times and committed an ocean operation to exercise local and delayed effects. Finished with $133.2M, last-four output 92.4%, incremental homeland revenue −$90.6M, peak dossier 46. The committee's recovery conditions were met despite substantial damage. The archive distinguishes programme survival from economic benefit; this preserves the game's dark institutional incentives rather than inventing a morality score. [Fire](fire.png), [recorded debrief](aggressive-archive.txt).
3. **Inactivity run.** No interventions; programme dissolved at review 12. Checked failure messaging and comparison with the preceding tenure. [Recorded debrief](inactivity-archive.txt).

Observed browser log warnings came from the MetaMask extension; no game frame or season exception was observed in these runs. The tests caught and prompted fixes for ignored clicks during fades/resolution and misleading harvest labels.

## Assessment and limits

The strongest improvement is that recovery, national trade exposure, wing rent and the eventual archive now give decisions concrete consequences. Forecasts remain partial intelligence, not guaranteed weather. The rival is legible and budgeted, but its policy is deliberately small. Some strategies remain simple; human playtests should judge whether the tradeoffs are compelling before further balance changes. Visual review covered seeded weather, fire, the globe, regional controls and the archive. Biological and permanent AMOC rules have automated coverage; their full late-century visual sequence was not played through by hand. This pass completes the six scoped changes, not a claim of award-level quality or exhaustive testing of every operation combination.
