Fields marked **authored** are the author's own: `dur` for every entry; the start month for multi-year droughts/famines; region assignment where the event straddles the prototype's map. Famine tolls use the conservative end of the mainstream range and say so. Tropical cyclones are excluded (HURDAT2/IBTrACS); Bhola 1970, Nina/Banqiao 1975 and Nargis 2008 are kept as their flood consequence only.
