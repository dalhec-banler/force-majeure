## Dropped after checking (author rule: consequential only)

- Awu 1966 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Awu — VEI 4 confirmed but the article gives no date or separate toll for 1966 (only an aggregate 11,048 across six eruptions); could not verify the toll.
- Fuego 1974 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volc%C3%A1n_de_Fuego — 15–21 Oct 1974, VEI 4 per list, "heavy agricultural losses" but unquantified and no toll; thin.
- Galeras 1993 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galeras — 14 Jan 1993, 9 dead (6 scientists, 3 tourists), VEI 2; verified but kept out to hold the count near the brief's ceiling.
- Spurr 1992 — https://avo.alaska.edu/eruption/crater-peak-1992-6 — 18 Aug 1992, VEI 3, Anchorage airport closed 20 h, no deaths; verified but kept out for count.
- Grímsvötn 2011 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gr%C3%ADmsv%C3%B6tn — 21 May 2011, VEI 4, 900 of 90,000 European flights cancelled; verified but minor beside Eyjafjallajökull; kept out for count.
